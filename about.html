About Julia

around practice.

Yoga as a daily meeting with what is real.

STILLNESS · STUDY · MOVEMENT · PRESENCE

Her approach

Practice is not
self-improvement.
It is contact.

Julia is a teacher of yoga, meditation, and qigong. Her work draws from classical yoga, pranayama, qigong, Vedic Tantra, functional movement, and contemplation — taught as living practices, not as labels.

She values precision without rigidity. You will be asked to work, to feel, and to notice. You will not be asked to perform a version of yourself.

Some days practice is quiet. Some days it has heat. The point is not to escape your life, but to meet it with a body and mind that are more available.

What guides the work

01

Craft

Clear, exact instruction and respect for the small details that change a practice.

02

Honesty

No performance of calm. Start with what is true and work from there.

03

Depth

Old practices approached with care, context, and enough time to let them work.
