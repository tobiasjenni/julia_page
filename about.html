Her approach

Practice is not
a performance.
It is relationship.

Julia is a yoga, meditation, and qigong teacher devoted to the intelligence of lived practice. Her approach brings together classical yoga, pranayama, qigong, Vedic Tantra, functional movement, and contemplative practice.

She teaches with both structure and sensitivity: clear direction, with room for each person’s own pace, history, and inner knowing.

Whether the practice is quiet or dynamic, the invitation remains the same — become present enough to hear what your body is already saying.
